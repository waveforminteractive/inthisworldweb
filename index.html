What it does

A complete studio, on your desk.

Everything you need to go from a blank stage to a finished story video — built for creators who want control and privacy.

🎬

Cinematic viewport

Orbit, frame and preview camera shots. Transform characters and props with gizmos, just like a real 3D editor.

🧍

Cast & characters

Built-in blocky characters and monsters, plus a character builder to color, shape and save your own cast.

🎙️

Offline narration

Local TTS voices (Piper when available, Windows SAPI otherwise) cached to disk. No cloud voices, ever.

🎵

Music & SFX

Bundled music tracks and sound effects, per-scene mixing, and ducking for narration.

🎞️

Timeline & storyboard

Drag-and-drop scenes, clips, shots and animations. Scrub the playhead across your whole story.

📤

MP4 export

Render frames locally and encode with bundled FFmpeg — H.264, AAC, faststart, optional subtitles.

🤖

Optional Director AI

Bring your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint for shot and scene suggestions. Disabled by default; key stays in OS secure storage.

🔒

Private by design

Core is 100% offline. No accounts, no telemetry, no CDN, no cloud. Your projects live in your userData folder.
